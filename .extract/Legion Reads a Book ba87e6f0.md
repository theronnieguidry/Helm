In and of itself it's in an oil pouch to slip into to protect it from water. In addition to that, it's made from special paper you're familiar with that's a little smoother and finer grain than normal parchment. It's resistant to water. \[Waterproof Journal]. Second, the journal doesn't have writing on all of the pages. It only has 1/4 of it that's written on. The remainder is blank. It doesn't look like this was a finished work. The next thing noticed is that this journal sometimes is thought of like a diary. Other things are poetic. During examination, at first blush, are not about things of great import.
