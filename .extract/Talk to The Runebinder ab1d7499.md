Check in with Lord Chilton's runesmith to see if I can get a rune upgrade for my bracers. He has a shop in the market square.

Entering the store I see a dwarven male in blousy ornate clothing with a black background and lots of red and gold embroidery on top. He's wearing a lot of rings; displaying a lot of wealth. He looks relatively young with fiery red hair. He's got a strong brogue dwarvish accent.

His name is Cadrick. I end up getting a discounted striking rune for my handwraps from him for 50g.
