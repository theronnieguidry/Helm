Fizzle and Bebe head to the market and hear that the young duke is going to bleed the city dry. They're complaining of taxation and the increases of them.  People are going to starve. People are angry about it. The other thing Fizzle hears is that now that we're close to the gatehouse, hanging along the bridge are birdcages. Some are filled with people of various ancestries. They're starving, filthy, dirty, and some could be dead. Some are moaning and barely moving (chests slowly rising and falling). The evenings are starting to cool off as in fall. Their skin is blistered, sunburnt, dried, cracked. There are wooden signs with burnt letters into them listing their crimes. "Theft, failure to pay tithes, etc." are the crimes listed. Not "Murder, Rape, etc."

Fizzle and I cross the bridge to the west side of town. The air is fresher here but the natural breeze the follows the pathway of the river clears the air a bit. We pass through the gatehouse integrated into the outer wall of the city. It's another level of hustle and bustle. The people here aren't well dressed. They're workers and laborers and the wagons that are crossing the river. They're heading to Stapleton's Coinhouse. There's a common area with fountains. The streets are cobblestoned including the alleyways. There aren't muddy streets here. It's more "modern" being the seat of ducal authority. The lowest of the merchant houses are nice looking and some with stucco painted white. The roofs here are clay or wood tiled. There are areas with statuary. There weren't many guards where we came from, but here they walk in pairs. It's more patrolled. The guards are in the gatehouses, not on the bridge. We see a sign for the Silver Horn, a tavern of decent composure. Flower boxes on the outside. Tables inside. Far nicer than the Wailing Wench. There's an inn called The Golden Grain inn. Fizzle asks me to take a deep sniff to see what food smells interesting. I smell fish/shellfish/butter/bread. We get a seat inside the Silver Horn. The lunch is pasta with local mussels out of the river and a butter cream sauce with cheese and herbs. We order two plates of this and a pot of tea. The nearby tables are also complaining about taxation. But we also hear excitement from vendors, shops that sell candles, materials people need for travel, soaps, oils and essences and perfumes, and maybe better things up and closer to the keep. Inside the keep wall are the actual castle, housing for the duke, guard houses, stables for the duke's private horses. We're between the outer and inner walls right now. They're excited about the tournament to sell their wares during the tournament. It's supposed to start within a couple days. A number of the bannermen - local, counts, barons, other small lords - maybe even some from outside the duchy would come to compete at this tournament. It's a big thing. It seems as though most of the people would be expected to compete or be here. However there's a lot of gossip and discussion about how people are surprised Baron Arnulf Chilton and none of his 3 sons are here. The baron was one of the best friends of the old duke. To not be here at the tournament the duke's son is throwing is surprising. The "young duke" "old duke" the old duke has died on a hunting trip on his birthday a few months ago. He was out with his son, the young duke, Steffen Von Skathen. The old duke was Geralt Von Skathen. Some think it's weird the young duke is having this tournament but others think it's normal due to the transition. There's gossip that it's weird that Baron Arnulf isn't here given his friendship with the old duke. There's rumors of an announcement made by the young duke at the tournament. They're expecting some new theatrical production at the Grand Skathen Theatre Complex. A new one must be being prepared to entertain the nobles during the tournament. Maybe even more than one! Our waiter introduces himself as Theodore. His family calls him Teddy. He's the oldest and some are still at home while others are apprentices. Brookies are a smoked white fish.

We head to the perfume and oil shop and I buy some oil in a 3 oz jar for 3s and 2 copper for the oil and the hair brush.
